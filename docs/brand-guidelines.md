## Brand idea

**Proof has gravity.**

Dimosthenis is an AI Software Engineer who works beyond prototypes: he can move from messy data and model behavior to a production surface that real people can understand and use.

## Positioning

For recruiters and engineering leaders looking for an early-career AI Software Engineer with unusually broad product ownership, Dimosthenis combines AI/data engineering, frontend execution, founder experience, and technical communication. The differentiation is inspectable work rather than a technology list.

## Voice

- Confident, not self-congratulatory.
- Specific, not adjective-heavy.
- Technical when it proves depth.
- Plain-language when it explains value.
- First person in primary portfolio copy.
- Evidence before claims.

Avoid:

- “Passionate developer.”
- “Innovative solutions.”
- “Cutting-edge technology.”
- “Transforming the future with AI.”
- Long technology inventories before project outcomes.

## Wordmark

- Website primary identifier: the full text `Dimosthenis Gkontolias`.
- Supporting descriptor: `AI Software Engineer`.
- No improvised `DG` square, badge, or icon is used as a primary logo.
- The existing favicon/icon may remain for browser metadata until a separate identity-mark project is approved.
- The wordmark is never placed inside a card-like container.
- It may switch between dark and light monochrome variants according to background.

## Color roles

- Orbit Ink: near-black spatial field; primary cinematic background.
- Warm Bone: principal text and editorial surface.
- Signal Acid: active focus, selected orbit trace, and key CTA only.
- Data Cyan: TalkToGreekData-specific signal.
- Product Orange: Quar-specific signal.
- Creator Coral: Demos Vibes-specific signal.
- Cloud Blue: TrackSights-specific signal.

Rules:

- Signal Acid never fills large background areas.
- Project colors identify ownership; they are not mixed into generic gradients.
- Glow is limited to physically plausible screen light and focus feedback.
- Text contrast must meet the declared WCAG target.

## Typography

- Instrument Serif: identity statements, large editorial gestures, and case-study titles.
- Manrope: body copy, navigation, CTAs, and product explanation.
- JetBrains Mono: coordinates, project indices, technical metrics, and interaction instructions.
- Type contrast comes from scale and role, not excessive weight variation.
- The largest display type should remain readable before becoming decorative.

## Imagery

- Use real product interfaces and authentic photography.
- Never substitute an abstract AI brain, robot, stock network globe, or generated fake dashboard for proof.
- Screens may be cropped, layered, lit, and placed in 3D, but their identity must remain recognizable.
- The portrait appears as a warm, layered human anchor inside the hero, not as a generic boxed headshot card.

## Spatial identity

- Projects are bodies with mass and a defined relationship to the candidate story.
- Orbital motion means exploration.
- Magnetic movement means focus.
- Front-facing alignment means inspection.
- Release back into orbit means returning to the full body of work.
- Spatial effects must always map to a real project, proof point, or action.

## Motion identity

- Character: deliberate inertia, restrained acceleration, soft magnetic settling.
- Never: bouncing UI, random floating, constant shimmer, rapid zoom, glitch, scan-line overload, or stock particle explosions.
- The user can interrupt, pause, or bypass continuous motion.
- Reduced motion preserves composition and meaning.

## UI geometry

- Large surfaces are planar and mostly square-edged or lightly rounded.
- Small labels use open typography rather than bordered pills.
- CTAs may use solid high-contrast geometry, but not a repeated generic rounded-button system.
- Dividers behave like trajectories or measurement marks.
- Repeated equal-weight cards are avoided.

## Consistency test

Every major element must answer at least one:

1. Which real project or proof does this represent?
2. Which recruiter decision does this help?
3. Which part of the orbit/focus/inspection grammar does this express?

If none apply, remove it.
